Workwrights is a skill you add to your AI. It brings a small hiring team of five agents: Scout, Evaluator, Editor, Researcher and Ellis. They help you find job listings, check how well you fit, sharpen your resume and keep going when the search gets hard.

## Quickstart

1. [Install Workwrights]({{ '/getting-started/install/' | relative_url }}) and run setup. It takes a few minutes.
2. Read about [the evidence bank]({{ '/getting-started/evidence-bank/' | relative_url }}). It's the heart of the system.
3. Meet [the agents]({{ '/agents/' | relative_url }}), then follow [the workflow]({{ '/workflow/' | relative_url }}) from search to application.

{% capture body %}Don't ask the AI to write your resume or cover letter for you. It will, and you'll lose the human quality that makes your application yours. Ask for suggested edits instead, and make them yourself.{% endcapture %}

## Your data 

As a jobseeker, you don't need third-party companies harvesting your career data as a product. With the Workwrights skill, you keep your own data. The skill holds only the logic (the agents and their rules) and blank templates. Your master resume, evidence bank, job ledger, reports and settings live in a workspace folder that **you** choose and control, whether a folder on your computer, a synced folder or a cloud drive. Nothing personal is ever written into the skill itself.

Every file is inspectable. You can see what's going on and change it if you want.

**Note for data privacy**: Your data will pass through your AI client. Check your settings and your AI client's policies on whether they retain your data.

## Works with your plan

This is all designed to work if you don't have premium tools. Paid plans save time, because the agents can search the web and schedule runs for you, but they aren't required. If you're on a free plan, use light mode to save on usage.

Need to customize something? Just do it. Tell your AI how you want things to work. It's yours.
