---
title: Salary ranges
lede: Know what to ask for before the application asks you.
---

Once you have some evidence and your resume, ask Researcher what an appropriate pay range would be for a specific job title. There may be more than one range; that's okay. Options are good.

Use these ranges to know what to ask for during the application and interview stages.

{% capture body %}When a prospective employer makes you an offer, ask: *"Is there a little wiggle room on the base?"* Recruiters often have some flexibility.{% endcapture %}
