title: Using a free plan
lede: How to get the most from Workwrights when your AI plan has tight usage limits.

Searching the web is the most demanding thing Workwrights does, and it can use a large share of a day's allowance on a free plan. Some free plans also limit browsing. Workwrights has a light search mode to help, and you don't need a paid plan for any of it.

## A free-plan workflow

1. Run setup, then say **"Find me jobs."** Scout asks whether you're on a free plan, and suggests light search if you are.
2. Ask Scout for titles from your resume, and for search terms and links for your job boards.
3. Open the links yourself and paste the postings that look promising.
4. Scout scores them with a short score and one line each. Ask [Evaluator]({{ '/agents/evaluator/' | relative_url }}) for full reports on your best two or three.
5. Ask [Editor]({{ '/agents/editor/' | relative_url }}) for suggested edits on those.

{% capture body %}A full Evaluator report reads your resume, your evidence bank and the whole posting, so it uses several times more of your allowance than a short score. Choose the listings that deserve one.{% endcapture %}

## Switching modes

- Say **"switch to light search"** to turn light search on.
- Say **"switch to full search"** to turn it off, for example after you upgrade.
- If a search stops because you hit a limit, you don't lose progress. Say **"continue my search"** when your allowance resets.

For more on how Scout works, see [Scout]({{ '/agents/scout/' | relative_url }}).
