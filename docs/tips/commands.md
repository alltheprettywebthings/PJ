---
title: Quick commands
lede: Things you can say to Workwrights, and what they do.
---

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "Set up my job search" | Runs setup: workspace, master resume, then optional extras. |
| "Find me jobs" | Scout asks for titles, location, seniority and boards, then searches. |
| "Show me my current prospects" | Lists the prospects in your tracker or ledger. |
| "Store that in my evidence bank" | Saves the experience you just described. |
| "I applied to the Acme role" | Scout updates that job's status in your tracker. |
| "Mark Initech as rejected" | Scout logs the rejection. |
| "Update my links" | Changes your portfolio and profile links. |
| "Set up my tracker" | Adds or changes your tracker. |
| "Run setup" | Goes through setup again. |
| "Switch to light search" | Scout writes search terms and links, and you paste postings back. Uses fewer tokens. |
| "Switch to full search" | Scout searches for you. |
| "Continue my search" | Scout picks up a search that stopped early. |
| "Switch to pre-written sentences" | Editor drafts wording from your evidence bank. |
| "Switch to suggested changes" | Editor tells you what to change and you write it. |
| "List edits in resume order" | Editor goes top to bottom through your resume. |
| "List edits by relevance" | Editor lists the edits that matter most to the job first. |
| "wrks" | After attaching a new version, shows what changed and any update options. |

## Talk to an agent by name

Start a message with an agent's name to send it to that agent: "Evaluator, give me a gap analysis on that listing."
