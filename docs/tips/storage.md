## Gather your ingredients

Before setup, make sure you have:

- Your master resume
- Something you've written that sounds like you
- An AI you want to use
- A plan for where your data will live: cloud, local, Airtable or something else

Then run setup. If the AI wants to name things a certain way or set up folders, you can go along with it, but you don't have to. There's a lot of flexibility in how you set up Workwrights.

## Folders and a tracker

Use a set of storage folders **and** a database or spreadsheet. For best results, keep these folders:

| Folder | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| Master | The master versions of your resume. Don't edit them unless you need to. |
| Drafts | When you start tailoring, tell the AI to **make a copy of** your master, put it here, and rename it for the job. |
| Deliverables | Put your finals here, and then drag them out when filling out job applications. You can also ask Evaluator to compare how your final scores against a job versus the master resume. |
| Archive | Old file versions, and resumes for jobs that sent a rejection. |

## Back it up: the 3-2-1 rule

Follow the 3-2-1 rule for important files:

- **3** copies in total
- **2** different types of storage
- **1** copy stored offsite
