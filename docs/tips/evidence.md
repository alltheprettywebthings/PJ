---
title: Find more evidence
lede: The more your evidence bank knows, the better Workwrights can match you to jobs and back up what you claim.
---

## From a gap analysis

When you run a gap analysis, Evaluator may ask whether you have anything that addresses the gaps. If you do, talk through that experience, then add it to your evidence bank.

## Any time

Thought of something else, or finished a big project at work? Add it to your evidence bank whenever you like:

> Store that in my evidence bank.

## From the tools you already use

If you record your work in Trello, Airtable, Notion or something similar, connect your AI to it. Then ask Workwrights to summarize that work and add the significant accomplishments to your evidence bank.
