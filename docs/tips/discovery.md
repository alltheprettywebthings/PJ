---
title: Find more jobs
lede: Widen your search by finding job titles you hadn't considered and the boards that list them.
---

Using Workwrights to find more possible job titles from your experience, then building a list of job boards for you or Scout to search, gives great results in later stages.

## An example workflow

<ol class="ww-steps">
<li><h3 class="ww-steps__title">Collect titles during setup</h3><p>Take the first round of job titles Workwrights suggests.</p></li>
<li><h3 class="ww-steps__title">Ask for job boards</h3><p>Ask Workwrights to recommend job boards for those titles.</p></li>
<li><h3 class="ww-steps__title">Track them</h3><p>Add the titles and boards to a spreadsheet, Airtable or similar, and keep adding boards as you find them.</p></li>
<li><h3 class="ww-steps__title">Run searches</h3><p>Ask Scout to search those boards, or run the searches yourself and give Scout a list of possible jobs.</p></li>
<li><h3 class="ww-steps__title">Revisit your titles</h3><p>As your evidence bank grows, Workwrights gets a better picture of all your work, not just what's on your resume. Ask for more job titles then. You don't want dozens; 3–5 good ones will probably give you solid results.</p></li>
</ol>

{% capture body %}Having Scout search for you takes tokens, so free plans may not manage it. You can still run searches yourself and hand Scout the results.{% endcapture %}
