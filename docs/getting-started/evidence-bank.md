## Two files, two jobs

- **Your master resume** is your static history.
- **Your evidence bank** is a growing, conversational collection of wins, metrics and context. At any point, you can tell an agent to add something to it.

The evidence bank starts empty on purpose. It + your resume are the only sources Workwrights uses for claims about you, so nothing gets invented.

## The goal

Every conversation with an agent should try to move a vague achievement on your resume into a measurable one in your evidence bank. Then you can see where you really score against a job listing.

For example, if you say "I increased traffic," Evaluator will ask: *"By what percentage? Over what timeframe?"* Once you answer, it asks to add the details to your evidence bank.

## Adding evidence

Be specific; numbers help. Pull fresh analytics if you can. Any time you remember something that isn't on your resume, tell any agent:

> Store that in my evidence bank.
