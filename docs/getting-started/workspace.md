## Where your files end up

Your workspace folder looks like this:

```text
job-search/
├── profile/        settings, evidence bank, target titles, saved searches
├── master/         your master resume (never modified)
├── job_search/     the ledger of listings, snapshots, run digests
├── drafts/         draft versions of your resumes and cover letters
└── deliverables/   tailored resumes and cover letters
```


{% capture body %}Workwrights doesn't rely on the AI to store your data in a chat. The AI can write and edit your files, but your data belongs to you. You can inspect it, write to it and stop using the system at any time, and your data will still be yours. It isn't stored anywhere else unless you let your AI use it. If you don't want that, turn the option off in your AI's settings; look under project memory, general settings or chat settings.{% endcapture %}

## Without a tracker

If you skip the tracker, the system still works. Scout shows prospects in the chat and keeps its own record in the ledger. Say **"set up my tracker"** whenever you want to add one.

## Check your files outside the AI

Before you lean on the system, open your workspace yourself:

- Can you read your spreadsheet or database?
- Can you find your master resume?
- Do you know where drafts will be saved?

The AI won't apply to jobs for you, so you need to know where these things are. For folder and backup advice, see [Set up your storage]({{ '/tips/storage/' | relative_url }}).
