Because Workwrights keeps each job's ID and URL, Researcher can pick up any listing from your ledger.

## What Researcher can find

- Scandals, controversies or legal cases you could get caught up in as an employee.
- What employees think of a company, from sources like Glassdoor.
- The right pay range for you. See [Salary ranges]({{ '/tips/salary/' | relative_url }}).
- Recent mergers and employment risks.

Researcher saves its reports to the `drafts/` folder in your workspace.

**Pro tip**: Set the research aside and ask Editor if any of it would help make a stronger cover letter.
