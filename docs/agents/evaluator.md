Evaluator points out soft gates, hard gates, hard requirements and reporting structures, and does a deep analysis of the listing. It also finds stories from your experience that could answer interview questions.

## Scores

- Evaluator rates on a 0–100 scale.
- 70 is a good match.
- Don't expect to get to 100.

It's strict with you too, and will point out small mismatches in your wording. That can be annoying, but it is consistent. It may argue about your word choice; that's part of its personality.

## Making listings more simple

Some postings bury the job in word salad, jargon, or long boilerplate. For those, Evaluator pulls out the concrete requirements (tools, years of experience, scope, deliverables) and sets them apart from the buzzwords. It describes the role in a few plain sentences and marks anything it inferred. It tells you how confident it is in the score, and why, and suggests questions to ask the recruiter. It scores on the concrete requirements only, so wording alone never raises or lowers a score.

**Even if the post is vague**, Evaluator and Editor will still suggest how to address gaps in your resume and cover letter in the same language (or semantically similar languagee) the posting uses, so simplifying job listings doesn't harm your chances.

## Prepare for interviews

Ask Evaluator to prepare interview questions for you. It uses your stories and writes behavioral questions.
