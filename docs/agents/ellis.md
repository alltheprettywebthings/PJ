---

Ellis listens to your worries and helps you reframe them with your own evidence. Ellis doesn't do toxic positivity, and gives you a safe space to talk things through. Your feelings are real. Job searches are hard.

Ellis can talk you through times when you feel burnt out, or like you aren't good enough for a job. Try:

> Ellis, I'm feeling burnt out by this job search.

> Ellis, I feel like an impostor.

{% capture body %}Ellis is not a therapist, and an AI is no substitute for one. If you need help, please seek professional help; it can be life-changing.{% endcapture %}
